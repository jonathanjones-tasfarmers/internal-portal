Emissions outcome (modelled)

Abatement is the share of methane the cattle are modelled not to produce because of KleenGrow. CO₂e converts methane into the same warming effect as carbon dioxide (1 kg of methane = 28 kg CO₂e).

      The record trail, from animal to result

      • ●
        Animals identified and weighed automatically
        OptiWeigh walk-over weighing links each liveweight to an animal and a mob, daily.
      • ●
        Each mob tied to its treatment
        Treatment is recorded by QR code at every lick block visit, so visits count by the treatment, not the unit.
      • ●
        Blocks put out and cost recorded
        Lick block visits are logged, giving blocks per 100 head per week and the cost per head.
      • ●
        Blocks sampled in the laboratory
        Bromoform is tested in the outer and inner layers of each block.
      • ●
        Pasture quality sampled
        Forage Lab Australia NIR analysis of paddock samples, 4 November 2026.
      • ●
        Modelled methane abatement calculated daily
        From block intake, using the method below.
      • ○
        Independent verification of abatement
        Not yet done. Abatement is modelled, not measured on the animal.

      The figures on this site are rebuilt from these records. Any correction to a record is backed up before it is applied.

      How comparisons are made

      • ✓KleenGrow and control mobs are compared over the same dates and the same number of days
      • ✓Daily ADG swings by hundreds of g/day, so the page counts the days on which the two mobs' 95% bands separate, not just the difference in means
      • !Mobs are not randomised: start weights, paddocks and start dates differ
      • !Intake is a supply measure: the records cannot show whether cattle would have eaten more if more blocks had been put out

      What is modelled and what is measured

      • ✓Measured: liveweight, ADG, blocks put out, bromoform in the blocks, pasture quality
      • !Modelled: methane abatement, CO₂e abated and its illustrative value
      • ✗Not yet measured: methane at the animal (AgScent), residues in beef, animal-health event counts
