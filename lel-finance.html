How these figures are worked out

Source. Xero is read every morning (read only; nothing is ever written to Xero). Budget, contributions and milestones are kept by hand in ArcGIS Online: open LEL_Fin_Budget, LEL_Fin_Contributions or LEL_Fin_Milestones and edit the cell.

Funds available is working capital: cash plus other current assets, less bills and other current liabilities. All of the $4M grant has already been received, so this is the money the Project has to finish with, before any partner cash still to come.

Average monthly spend uses the last 6 complete months. If wages or large invoices have not been posted for some months, it will look low and the money will look like it lasts longer than it will.

Budget to date spreads each calendar year's Agreed Budget evenly across its 12 months, from January 2025. Spending from June to December 2024 counts against the total but there was no 2024 budget line, so early lines can look ahead of budget.

Partner cash received sums the income account named for each partner in LEL_Fin_Contributions. Partners with no Xero account named there show "not in Xero".
