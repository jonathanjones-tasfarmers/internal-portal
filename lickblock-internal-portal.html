How this is calculated

KleenGrow™ is the Sea Forest product in the lick blocks. The Grant Deed and earlier reports call it SeaFeed™; both names mean the same product. The data layers still label it “seafeed”.

Intake is grams of block per head per day over the latest interval between two visits to the same trough or unit. The whole-cohort figure is all block mass placed since the cohort started, per head per day.

On hand is blocks ordered less blocks used. Days to empty divides on hand by the current use rate. Reorder by allows a 40-day lead time.

Sources: LEL_Lick_Block_Usage_History (fed daily by 06. Lick_Block_Portal_Feed.py) · LEL_LickBlock_Placements · orders logged directly in ArcGIS Online (LEL_Lick_Block_Orders).
