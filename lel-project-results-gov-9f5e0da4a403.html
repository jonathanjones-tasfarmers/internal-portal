Intake is what was put out, so the records cannot show whether cattle would have eaten more.

Emissions intensity is modelled methane over the same days as the weight gain, as CO₂e, divided by that gain. It covers the Project period only, not a whole-of-life footprint. Measured methane at the animal is not yet included.

Productivity

Rolling 14-day ADG with 95% band (g/day)

ADG is average daily gain. The shaded bands are the 95% range: where the two bands overlap, the mobs cannot be told apart.

KleenGrow minus control: weight difference (kg)

Above zero, the KleenGrow mob is heavier on average; below zero, the control mob is heavier.

Mobs are compared over the same dates and the same number of days. Mobs are not randomised, so start weights and grazing differ.
