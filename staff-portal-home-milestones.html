Where the Project stands

Key messages so far

  • Neither farm shows a consistent growth benefit from KleenGrow yet.
  • KleenGrow cost 41% to 54% less than budgeted at the two completed cohorts.
  • Cattle eat about half the dose the methane model assumes, so modelled abatement is small.
  • Results are early: mobs are not randomised and there is no full finishing period yet.

What comes next

  • Cohort 2 at Bowood and Eastbourne builds to a 30-day matched window.
  • OptiWeigh installed at TRT Pastoral, and first weight data.
  • Measured methane (AgScent) to test the modelled abatement.
  • A full finishing period before any economic conclusion.
