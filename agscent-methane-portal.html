      How to read this

      What this data is, and what it is not

      • The sensor samples an animal's breath during voluntary visits to the OptiWeigh unit. It is a short window, so it ranks animals against each other better than it gives an absolute daily total.
      • Only visits where AgScent found a methane peak get an emission estimate. Longer visits are far more likely to, so the estimates describe those visits, not every visit.
      • Estimates are in grams of CH4 per day. AgScent's export does not say which method (Bell or Madsen) produced them, or whether the AVG or MAX column is the one to use, and the two give very different results.
      • Expected methane is Charmley's equation applied to each animal's weight. TIA found this same sensor reads roughly a third of that on untreated animals, so a measured figure well below 100% is not by itself proof of SeaFeed's effect.
      • Individual visits vary a lot. Use animal figures only when marked Reliable, and herd trends only where the week has enough visits.
      • All readings are from SeaFeed-fed animals. Bowood is the only farm with a unit, so there is no control group in this data and no comparison to draw from it.
