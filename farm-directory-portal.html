How this is calculated

KleenGrow™ is the Sea Forest product in the lick blocks. The Grant Deed and earlier reports call it SeaFeed™; both names mean the same product. The data layers still label it “seafeed”.

Farms come from lick block usage (LEL_Lick_Block_Usage_History) and the Farm Directory table. A farm’s later cohorts (“… - Cohort 2”) show on that farm’s card, not as farms of their own. Contact details stay private in ArcGIS Online (LEL_Farm_Directory) and are edited there when signed in, along with species, committed head, stage and go-live quarter.

Pasture quality is Forage Lab Australia NIR analysis of paddock samples for each mob (LEL_Pasture_Quality): crude protein and aNDF (fibre) as % of dry matter, and metabolisable energy (ME) in MJ per kg of dry matter. Higher protein and ME, and lower fibre, mean better feed. One sampling is a snapshot of that day only.

A visit is overdue once its next-visit date has passed. Targets are cumulative farms and head per quarter from LEL_Onboarding_Targets, seeded from the funded budget and edited directly in ArcGIS Online. A farm counts as committed when its stage is Active, or when it has lick block usage and no stage set.
