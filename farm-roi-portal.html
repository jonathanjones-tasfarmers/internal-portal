The producer contribution rate is not a fixed charge. It's tied to the Producer Co-Contribution Principle: producers are only asked to contribute if the project can show a productivity, performance or price benefit of at least equivalent value. The "SeaFeed gaining Xg/day" stat, the cost-accrual chart, the ahead/behind line and the monthly snapshot strip all read real history from LEL_Economics_Data (cumulative_cost_aud and weight_gain_kg_per_head, one row per farm/treatment per day since that farm's trial start): genuine SeaFeed cost accrued from real lick blocks fed out and real observed weight gain, not a projection, and this is the page's only "ahead or behind" verdict. The farmer/grant split on the cost chart, and every "net" figure, apply each card's own Contribution rate (%) assumption to that real cost: edit it in Adjust assumptions if the project-wide rate changes, and every real-data figure on the card updates together. Sale price (used to value the ADG advantage) auto-fills from the MLA National Heavy Steer Indicator: edit it once and this page stops touching it. The separate "Modelled annual estimate" block on each card is a projection from the assumptions in Adjust assumptions (dose × price × visitation × 365), useful for "what would justify this" conversations, but it won't match the real cumulative figure above it unless every assumption happens to hold exactly, and it carries no verdict of its own. SeaFeed cost, contribution rate and benefit-pathway defaults in that modelled estimate reproduce the live TLEL Project Budget model (Supplement Costs, Farmer Break-Even, Project Budget sheets, as at 8 Sep 2026). Head counts default to each farm's 2026 deployment from the Beef Farm Head/Mob table: edit them to match what's actually on the ground when you're talking to a farmer. Dose auto-fills per farm from the Lick Block Tracker's own measured consumption rate (LEL_Lick_Block_Usage_History): edit it once and this page stops touching it.
