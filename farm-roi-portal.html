Farm ROI

What each cattle trial farm's SeaFeed co-contribution comes to at its current head count, and what needs to come back to the farmer to justify it. Built for conversations in the paddock — one card per farm, edit head count and assumptions on the spot.

The producer contribution rate is not a fixed charge. It's tied to the Producer Co-Contribution Principle: producers are only asked to contribute if the project can show a productivity, performance or price benefit of at least equivalent value. The 60.23% figure below is the current project-wide rate (Project Budget!D86) — use it as the working number for a conversation, not a guarantee the farmer owes it.
